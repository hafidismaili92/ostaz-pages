ProfKit

Privacy policy

App: ProfKit — com.profkit.app
Last updated: 26 November 2026

In short

  • As of today, ProfKit contains no advertising and no trackers.
  • Your data is never sold, rented or shared for commercial purposes.
  • You alone see your classes: every account is isolated at the database level.
  • Deleting your account is immediate and irreversible.

1. Who does what

ProfKit is a tool that you fill in yourself. This distinction governs everything else:

  • For your students' data, it is you, or the school that employs you, who decides what is recorded and why. We only host and return what you write. In GDPR terms: you are the controller, ProfKit is the processor.
  • For your own account data, the publisher of ProfKit is the controller.

In practice: it is up to you to make sure you are allowed to record your students' information in a digital tool — your school's rules, informing families, approval from your management. ProfKit cannot do that for you.

2. What ProfKit processes

About you

  • Account: email address and password (encrypted, never readable by us)
  • Profile: name, school, school address and city, position, language
  • Career history: schools, cities, roles and years, if you fill them in
  • Tutoring: duration, fees and payment status of sessions, if you use that feature

About your students, entered by you

  • Identity: first name, last name, and gender if you fill it in
  • A parent's contact details: phone or email, if you fill them in
  • Schoolwork: grades, assessments, averages, attendance, absences, late arrivals, homework and submissions
  • Free text: behaviour observations, behaviour points and their reason, report card comments, personal notes

Free-text fields deserve your attention: they are the only places where you can write anything you like, including sensitive information. We do not read them and do not analyse them, but we cannot control their content either. Write there what you would write in an official document.

3. What ProfKit does not do

  • No loading from third-party servers: the typefaces are bundled inside the app
  • No advertising, no trackers, no analytics tools
  • No selling or renting of data
  • No profiling, no automated decision-making
  • No marketing email — only account emails (confirmation, forgotten password) are sent
  • No access to your photos, your camera, your contacts or your location
  • No transmission to any artificial-intelligence service: automatic report comment generation exists in the code but is not enabled

4. Where the data is hosted

ProfKit relies on Supabase for authentication and the database. The servers used are hosted by AWS in London (region eu-west-2), in the United Kingdom.

  • For users in the European Union: the data leaves the EU, but the United Kingdom benefits from an adequacy decision of the European Commission. No additional contractual safeguards are therefore required.
  • For users in Morocco: the data is hosted outside Morocco.

5. Who else sees anything

The list is short, and there is no other:

  • Supabase — host of the database and of authentication. Acts on our instructions and does not use the data for its own purposes.
  • WhatsApp or your SMS app — only when you choose to contact a parent. ProfKit prepares the message and opens it in the app you have installed; you are the one who sends it, from your own account, and the content is then governed by that service's terms.

6. How long

  • Your data is kept for as long as your account exists.
  • When the account is deleted, everything is erased immediately: no grace period, no trash, no recovery window.
  • Nothing is kept for commercial or statistical purposes after deletion.

The full procedure is described on the Delete your account page.

7. Security

  • All communications are encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Isolation between accounts is enforced by the database itself, not by the application: every row carries its owner, and a security rule prevents reading anyone else's. Even a programming mistake cannot expose another teacher's classes.
  • Passwords are stored encrypted and are never readable.

8. Your rights

You can access your data, correct it, erase it, obtain a copy of it, or object to its processing.

  • Access and correction: directly in the app, at any time.
  • A copy of your data: CSV export of grades and attendance is available from each class.
  • Erasure: Settings → Delete my account, or by email.

For any request: profkit-contact@hafidonline.org

9. If you are a student or a parent

The information about you was entered by a teacher, as part of their work. It is the teacher — or their school — who decides what is recorded.

Please contact the teacher or the school first. We have no way of knowing which teacher a request relates to, and we do not look at the contents of accounts. If you get no answer, write to us and we will make the connection.

10. Minors

ProfKit is intended for adult teachers. The app is not meant to be used by students, and no student account exists.

The recorded data does, however, concern minors. That is precisely why nothing is shared with third parties, nothing is used for advertising, and deletion is total and immediate.

11. Changes

This policy describes what ProfKit does today, as of the date shown at the top of this page. It may change along with the app.

Any change will be published here with a new date. And if a change were to affect the nature of the data processed or who receives it — for example the introduction of advertising or of an analytics tool — you would be informed in the app before it takes effect, and your consent would be requested where the law requires it.

Two commitments will not change: the school data you enter will never be sold or rented, and it will never be used to target advertising.

12. Morocco — Law 09-08

The publisher of ProfKit is established in Morocco. The processing of account data therefore falls under Law No. 09-08 on the protection of individuals with regard to the processing of personal data, and under the supervision of the CNDP.

  • Your rights: access, rectification and objection are exercised at the address given in section 13, under the conditions set out in Law 09-08.
  • Transfer outside Morocco: hosting is located in the United Kingdom (section 4). This transfer is necessary in order to provide the service you ask for: without remote hosting, the app cannot work. The formalities required by the CNDP are being completed.

If you record student data, you are yourself creating a personal data file. Depending on your situation — independent teacher, or employee of a school — the corresponding formalities may fall to you, or to your school. ProfKit can neither carry them out on your behalf nor take your place in doing so.
