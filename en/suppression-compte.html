ProfKit

Delete your account and your data

App: ProfKit — com.profkit.app

From the app

This is the fastest way, and the deletion is immediate.

  1. Open ProfKit and sign in
  2. Go to Settings
  3. At the very bottom, tap Delete my account
  4. Enter your email address to confirm

By email

If you no longer have access to the app, write to profkit-contact@hafidonline.org from the account's email address, with “Account deletion” as the subject.

Requests are handled on receipt. If the request does not come from the account's email address, we first have to verify your identity, which takes a little longer.

What is deleted

Everything, permanently and with no way to recover it:

  • Your account and your teacher profile
  • Your classes, subjects and levels
  • Your students, including their names and their parents' contact details
  • Grades, assessments and report cards
  • Attendance and absences
  • Homework, chapters and the lesson book
  • Observations and behaviour points
  • The timetable and tutoring sessions

When?

Immediately. As soon as you confirm, the account and all the data listed above are erased. There is no grace period, no trash and no recovery window: the action cannot be undone.

Nothing is kept for commercial purposes.

Before you delete

Remember to export anything you want to keep. ProfKit can export grades and attendance as CSV from each class.

Any questions? profkit-contact@hafidonline.org
